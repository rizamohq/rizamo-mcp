# Rizamo

Run your social accounts from Claude. Rizamo publishes and schedules posts on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn, YouTube and Google Business Profile, shows how they did, and answers comments and messages.

## What you can ask for

- Publish now or schedule reels, carousels, stories, videos and feed posts. Rizamo checks each network's media and text rules before a post goes out, and each profile has its own queue of posting times.
- See views, reach, engagement and followers for any period against the one before, every post ranked, and who your audience is.
- Read open comments and messages in one list, reply, hide or delete comments, and send a private message to people who comment a keyword.

## You stay in charge

Claude shows you what it will do and waits for your yes before it publishes, replies, deletes or connects a paid account, unless you ask it to act on its own. You choose which profiles it can reach when you sign in, and you can remove its access at any time.

## What the plugin adds and sends

The plugin adds one remote MCP server, `https://mcp.rizamo.com/mcp`, which Rizamo runs. Nothing is installed or run on your machine. You sign in to Rizamo with OAuth. Claude then sends Rizamo what you ask for, such as a post's text and media or a reply, and gets back your accounts, posts, analytics, comments and messages.
